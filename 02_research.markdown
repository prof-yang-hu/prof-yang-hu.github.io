## Research

My research examines family and work changes and inequalities in a global context. If you have difficulty accessing any of the full texts, please get in touch.

### [Family & Work Inequality](/research/family-inequality/)
How social, cultural, economic and institutional changes, as well as global mobilities, shape family relations and inequalities.

### [Digitalisation of Family & Work](/research/digi-fam-work/)
The implications of digitalisation and AI for family and work changes and inequalities, funded by the ESRC (UK) and SSHRC (Canada).

### [COVID-19 & Disasters](/research/covid-19-disasters/)
The social and mental health impacts of COVID-19 and disasters/crises, and their inequality implications.

### [Book](/research/book/)
*Chinese-British Intermarriage: Disentangling Gender and Ethnicity* (Palgrave Macmillan, 2016).
