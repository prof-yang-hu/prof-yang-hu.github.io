## Digitalisation of Family and Work Lives

Digitalisation is rapidly transforming our everyday family and work lives. My recent and ongoing research examines the implications of digitalisation for family and work changes and inequalities. This line of research is funded by the Economic and Social Research Council (UK) and the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council (Canada).
